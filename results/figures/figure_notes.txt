Figure 1: detection_threshold.csv; known-frame detection from 100 seeded trials per cell on a 100 px torso. The outlined 3 px column has detection rates 15%, 46%, 98%, 100% for 5%, 10%, 20%, 40% breaks respectively. It supports reliable detection of the larger breaks, not all breaks.
Figure 2: medians recalculated from pose_error.csv and pose_error_nsaqa.csv with head excluded. Missing predictions have no numeric error and are omitted from medians. Finite gross failures remain included. Counts and exact plotted medians are in figure_2_phase_medians.csv. Phases shown are the five requested phases.
Figure 3: model medians are the user-supplied rounded head-excluded values in model_medians.csv. Human 7.8 px comes from human_consistency.csv, 47 paired points across 6 frames. Human self-disagreement is not identical to model-versus-label error; comparable magnitudes are consistent with image ambiguity but do not establish a hard performance ceiling. The human overall was supplied without a head-exclusion specification.
Human ranges are stored as ranges, not invented midpoint estimates. Head 5 px is flagged approximate. Subset counts were not supplied and remain empty.
